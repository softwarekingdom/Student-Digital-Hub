PLEASE READ BEFORE CHATTING

Community Chat Rules

📜
  1. Personal Data Privacy: Do not share personal information such as your home address, phone number, school timetable, passwords, verification codes, or financial details. Student Digital Hub is not responsible for misuse or harm arising from information you voluntarily disclose, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
  2. Respectful Communication: Do not use unnecessary, abusive, insulting, sexually inappropriate, or otherwise unsuitable language.
  3. No Harassment or Bullying: Threats, bullying, intimidation, stalking, hate speech, and repeated unwanted messages are not allowed.
  4. No Harmful or Illegal Content: Do not send content intended to threaten, exploit, defraud, or harm another person, or content that violates applicable law.
  5. Respect Consent: A private chat may begin only after both students accept the request. Respect a student's decision to decline, stop, or leave a conversation.
  6. Block and Report: Use the Block or Report controls if you experience or notice abuse. Reports may require review when the reporting service is available.
  7. Protect Account Security: Never share passwords, one-time codes, login links, or another person's account details.
  8. Temporary Messages: Chat messages are not intended to be stored permanently. Messages may be deleted when either participant leaves the chat or when the server restarts. Do not assume messages can be recovered.
  9. No Impersonation: Do not pretend to be another student, use another person's ID, or mislead others about who you are.
  10. Report Misuse: Report inappropriate conduct and leave unsafe conversations. Blocking or reporting does not guarantee that all misuse can be prevented.
  11. Respect Others' Privacy: Do not copy, publish, or forward another person's messages or personal information without their permission.
  12. Use the Service Responsibly: Do not spam, send malicious links, attempt to access another student's chat, or interfere with the service.
