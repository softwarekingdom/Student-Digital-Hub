REQUIRED COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Group Chat Rules

📜
  1. Personal Data Privacy: Do not share your home address, phone number, school timetable, passwords, verification codes or financial information. Student Digital Hub is not responsible for misuse or harm arising from information you voluntarily disclose, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
  2. Respectful Communication: Do not use unnecessary, abusive, insulting, sexually inappropriate or otherwise unsuitable language.
  3. No Harassment or Bullying: Threats, bullying, intimidation, stalking, hate speech and repeated unwanted messages are prohibited.
  4. No Harmful or Illegal Content: Do not share content intended to threaten, exploit, defraud or harm another person, or content that violates applicable law.
  5. Respect Privacy: Do not publish, forward or expose another student's personal information or messages without permission.
  6. No Impersonation: Do not pretend to be another student or mislead members about your identity.
  7. No Spam or Malicious Links: Do not flood the room with messages, send suspicious links or attempt to disrupt the service.
  8. Respect Consent: Do not pressure anyone to join a room, reply to a message or share personal information.
  9. Block and Report: Report inappropriate conduct and block users when these features are available. Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern.
  10. Temporary Messages: Messages are not intended to be stored permanently. They may be deleted when a participant leaves the room or the server restarts.
  11. Keep Conversations Appropriate: Group chat is for respectful student interaction and learning. Content that makes others feel unsafe or uncomfortable is not allowed.
  12. Use the Service Responsibly: Do not attempt to bypass access restrictions, view hidden student data or access a group without authorization.
